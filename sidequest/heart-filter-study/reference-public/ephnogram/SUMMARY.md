# EPHNOGRAM ECG heart-rate reference

Source: EPHNOGRAM v1.0.0 (PhysioNet), DOI 10.13026/tjtq-5911, Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0. Generated by `src/ecg_reference.py` from the ECG channel only.

## Method

- Detector 1: Pan-Tompkins style, zero-phase: 10-25 Hz band-pass (above the classic 5-15 Hz because tall exercise T waves dominated that band), derivative, squaring, centred 150 ms moving integration. Peaks at least 0.25 s apart above noise + 0.25 x (beat level - noise), with beat level the 10 s running median of 2 s maxima and noise the 10 s running median. Search-back for gaps over 1.66 x the local median interval at half threshold; a peak less than 0.36 s after a beat with under half its maximum slope is dropped as a T wave.
- Detector 2: Wavelet and matched template: geometric mean of the Mexican-hat wavelet moduli at 6, 10 and 16 ms scales gives QRS candidates; the strongest candidates of each 60 s block form a median QRS template (+-60 ms, 5-40 Hz ECG). Beats are peaks of the normalised cross-correlation with that template above 0.6, at least 0.25 s apart, whose wavelet product within +-30 ms reaches 0.3 x the median of the template beats (rejects T waves, which match in shape but lack fine-scale energy), with search-back down to correlation 0.35 and wavelet ratio 0.2 for gaps over 1.6 x the local median interval.
- Refinement: Each detection is refined to the local maximum, within +-75 ms, of the 5-40 Hz zero-phase band-passed ECG at 1 kHz multiplied by the dominant QRS sign of its 60 s block (sign of the skewness of that signal, computed independently of both detectors and shared by them), with parabolic interpolation.
- Windows as in run 1 (`src/reference_beats.py`): 8 s, one ending at every whole second, rate = 60 x (beats - 1) / (last - first beat time). Time zero is the record start (nothing discarded), so the first window ends at 8 s. `reference_bpm` is the mean of both detector rates.
- Reliable window: detectors agree within 2.0 BPM; every beat of both detectors in the window matched by the other within 50 ms; all intervals 0.27-2.0 s; no interval above 1.4 x or below 0.7 x the window median; no gap over 1.3 x median at the window edges; no run of 8 ECG samples at the 16-bit limits (within 1.5 LSB); no second whose 5-15 Hz ECG standard deviation is below 0.1 x the record median.
