Block diagram of the device's audio chain: the microphone (16,000 values per second, 24 bit) feeds a listening path (filter switch Raw / Murmur / BPM filter, volume 0–100 %, 24-bit output limit, converter and earpieces) and a heart-rate path (BPM filter always on, loudness curve at 100 values per second, last 8 s kept, repeat-pattern search once per second, beats per minute). The two amber boxes are the filters studied here.
