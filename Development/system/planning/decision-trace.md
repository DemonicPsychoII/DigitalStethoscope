# Decision trace

Requirements, architecture, design and code state what is. They do not say who decided something,
when, or what was measured to get there. This table keeps that record: one row per decision,
measurement, review or own source behind a statement in those documents.

- **Statement** is the rule as the product document states it after the document cleanup of
  November 2026.
- **Record** repeats what the documents said before the cleanup, with its date and the people
  named. It adds nothing to that text. Where the documents linked a decision record instead of
  writing the date out, the row gives the date of that record.
- **Existing log record** links the decision record in this folder that already holds the decision,
  or says `none` when this row is the only record.
- **Documents** are relative to `Development/system/`.

This folder is not transferred to GitLab, and product documents do not link here. The lab
deployment row leaves out the host name and the network address of the test server.
